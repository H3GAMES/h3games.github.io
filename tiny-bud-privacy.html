Privacy Policy

Tiny Bud Adventures by H-Cube Inc · Effective date: 21 November 2026

H-Cube Inc ("we", "us") made Tiny Bud Adventures (the "Game"), a mobile game for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains what information is handled while you play.

1. Information we collect

  • The Game does not ask for, collect or store any personal information: no name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • The Game has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features.
  • Level progress and music and sound settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

2. Advertising

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to a "G" content rating.
  • Ads are not personalised. The Game does not collect or use the Android Advertising ID, and does not use remarketing.
  • AdMob may automatically collect limited technical data: IP address (used for approximate location), device type, operating system, app set ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic and crash information.
  • Ads appear only between plays (after all lives are lost) and can be closed.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The Game is designed to comply with COPPA, the EU GDPR and Google Play's Families Policy. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party involved is Google AdMob.

5. Data security and retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game.

6. Your choices

Uninstalling the Game stops all data processing by it. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

7. Changes to this policy

Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
