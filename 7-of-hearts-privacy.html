Privacy Policy

7 of Hearts (Laal Paan Satti) by H-Cube Inc · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the 7 of Hearts app ("the game") uses and why. The game is a single-player card game played against computer opponents.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your settings (player names, avatars, theme, text size, sound and vibration choices), your coin balance and your scores are saved only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you can watch to earn in-game coins and an ad after a match. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent for personalised ads when it first starts. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-game coins

Coins are virtual and have no real-money value. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money or prizes.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
